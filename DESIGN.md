## Brand & Style
The design system crafts an enchanted, bedtime cyber-lullaby environment for digital nurseries and nocturnal companions. It bridges cozy childhood comfort with neon-tinted celestial interfaces—an atmosphere of twilight tranquility, dreamy wonder, and protective warmth.

### Visual Style: Ethereal Luminous Glassmorphism
The visual architecture blends deep, velvety nightfall with translucent, luminous frosted planes. Interfaces feel like suspended observation windows looking out onto a tranquil constellation:
- **Translucent Depth:** Nested glass containers layered over deep atmospheric dark space.
- **Bioluminescent Accents:** Soft glows in pastel neon pink, celestial cyan, and star yellow highlight interactions and metrics without harsh glare.
- **Dream-Capsule Geometry:** Softly curved capsule pills, organic cloud contours, and sparkling star flourishes that soften structured technical controls.

## Colors
The palette balances protective twilight depths with high-clarity pastel neon accents, engineered for late-night legibility and soothing low-light viewing.

### Palette Architecture
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - `canvas-midnight`: `#0F1123` — The infinite cosmic background.
  - `surface-twilight`: `#161938` — Elevated surface foundation for primary panels.
  - `surface-violet`: `#241B48` — Active, high-interaction containers, alerts, and glowing docks.
- **Accents & Illumination:**
  - `primary` (Pastel Neon Pink): `#FF94B9` — Core CTAs, key focus states, heartbeat monitors, and emotional status signals.
  - `secondary` (Celestial Cyan): `#8BE9FD` — Connectivity markers, atmospheric metrics, telemetry badges, and ambient cooling/audio indicators.
  - `tertiary` (Glowing Star Yellow): `#FFE066` (Primary) & `#FFD166` (Deep) — Nightlights, sleep quality ratings, milestones, and celestial alerts.
- **Text & Structure:**
  - `text-primary` (Cloud White): `#F8FAFC` — Crisp headers and high-priority data points.
  - `text-secondary` (Stardust Lavender): `#B4B8DE` — Supportive copy, timestamps, and secondary indicators.
  - `border-glass`: `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.12)` — Delicate frosted rims catching ambient neon light.

## Typography
Typography marries warm whimsy with technological precision:
- **Headlines (Quicksand):** Playful, rounded forms evoke warmth and storybook comfort while remaining structurally grounded.
- **Body Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Highly legible, humanistic sans-serif providing optimal readability on glowing backdrops during nocturnal feedings or sleep tracking.
- **Metrics & Capsule Labels (Space Grotesk):** Gives telemetry chips, sensor readouts, and timestamps a clean cyber-nursery identity.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-column system with generous internal padding, ensuring elements breathe like slow, rhythmic sleep cycles.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters. Single-column stacked cards for swift one-handed navigation.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Dual-pane dream-capsule feeds and telemetry panels.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid capped at `1280px` max content width with `2rem` (32px) outer margins, featuring an asymmetric control center and constellation status overview.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is built using tinted frosted glass layers and colored ambient back-glows rather than murky, dark drop-shadows.

### Layer Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Layer (Base):** Deep `#0F1123` backdropped by subtle radial neon gradients (`#241B48` at 40% blur).
2. **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Trays):** Background `rgba(22, 25, 56, 0.65)`, backdrop blur `16px`, bordered with `1px solid rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.08)`.
3. **Surface Level 2 (Floating Capsule Cards):** Background `rgba(36, 27, 72, 0.70)`, backdrop blur `24px`, bordered with `1px solid rgba(255, 148, 185, 0.25)`.
4. **Surface Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Background `rgba(15, 17, 35, 0.88)`, backdrop blur `32px`, bordered with `1px solid rgba(139, 233, 253, 0.40)`.

### Luminescent Glow Tokens
- **Pink Aura:** `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(255, 148, 185, 0.28)`
- **Cyan Nebula:** `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(139, 233, 253, 0.24)`
- **Starlight Bloom:** `0 4px 20px 0px rgba(255, 224, 102, 0.35)`

## Shapes
The design system embraces ultra-soft, organic, and pill-shaped aesthetics. Standard edges are eradicated in favor of soft, cloud-like radii:
- **Pill Form Factors (Full Curvature):** Applied to buttons, category tags, notification chips, and input bars.
- **Capsule Panels (`rounded-xl` / 48px):** Modular cards, nightlight dashboards, and nursery sensor pods employ deep curving corners that evoke physical baby monitors and orbital pods.
- **Micro-Accents:** 4-point sparkle stars (`✦`) and miniature crescent motifs serve as visual anchors for section breaks and selection indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Glow Button:** Capsule pill shape. Filled with vibrant pastel pink (`#FF94B9`), label text in `#0F1123` with bold weight. Casts a soft pink aura shadow. On hover, scales slightly (102%) with starlight yellow boundary highlight.
- **Secondary Glass Button:** Capsule shape. Background `rgba(36, 27, 72, 0.6)`, text in celestial cyan (`#8BE9FD`), border `1px solid rgba(139, 233, 253, 0.35)`.
- **Icon Glow Buttons:** Circular glass nodes (`44px x 44px`) housing rounded line icons with subtle starlight tooltips.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Status Capsules:** Compact pills (`font: label-capsule`) featuring a blinking bioluminescent dot (cyan for sleeping, pink for active feeding, yellow for waking). Background `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.05)` with glowing boundary rims.

### Cards & Pods
- **Telemetry & Audio Cards:** Frosted glass panels with `28px` corner radius. Header displays quick stats with Space Grotesk numerals, accompanied by whimsical floating star icons (`✦`). Background features soft dynamic gradient blooms matching the active mode.

### Inputs & Sliders
- **Dream Search & Text Inputs:** Pill containers filled with `rgba(22, 25, 56, 0.8)`, padded with `1rem 1.5rem`. Placeholder text in `#B4B8DE`. Focus state transitions the border to celestial cyan with an outer glow ring.
- **Lullaby & Light Sliders:** Thick pill track (`12px` height) with soft indigo groove; active progress line filled with pastel neon gradient (pink to cyan); thumb is an illuminated star-yellow orb.

### Checkboxes & Toggles
- **Toggles:** Pill-shaped capsules. Inactive state is deep midnight with an off-white thumb; active state fills with vibrant gradient pink and shifts thumb with an animated starburst flash.
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squares (`8px` radius) with subtle cyan borders; checked state fills with pink containing a cloud-white checkmark.

### Specialized Component: Constellation Sleep Tracker
- Floating timeline capsule combining audio waveform bars rendered as miniature glowing starbeams alongside sleep-cycle depth markers.
